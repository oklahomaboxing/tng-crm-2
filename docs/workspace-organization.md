# Workspace organization

Navigation is grouped into Home, Gym & Members, Boxing & Events, Shop & Sales, Marketing, and Administration. Existing workspace names, URL hashes, event/member deep links and role permissions are retained. Promoter and TNG Boxing Events remain separate existing workflows. The Auto-Match Tracker stays at the bottom of Promoter’s Matchmaking page.

The sidebar shows compact role-appropriate Daily Tools shortcuts and expandable sections. Navigating opens the current section and Home, reducing menu clutter. Sidebar search also recognizes task terms and clears when a workspace is selected. Global search (Ctrl/Cmd K) groups results by business area and supports terms such as shirts, signed contracts, front desk and expired SMS. Search and shortcuts are always filtered by the existing role menu.

The dashboard retains existing live metrics, event information, work queues, attendance and sales data. Its workspace directory gives each business area a card with tool descriptions and optional additional links. Operational priorities appear above the directory. The top bar identifies the current business area. No new backend jobs, payment logic, account access, data migrations or messaging actions are introduced.
