# Unfinished checkout investigation and repair — November 14, 2026

The live review found 23 pending records across the system, 17 for Fall Brawl 6. Eight event attempts had another paid purchase matched by email or normalized phone. These matches are review flags, not proof that the unpaid attempt was replaced: a customer may have intended to buy additional tickets. No historical attempt was deleted, merged, marked paid, or automatically charged.

A read-only Clover lookup returned 352 merchant orders, below its requested page limit. No successful Fall Brawl line-item payment was found without an assigned TNGOS payment reference. The remaining event candidates had no same-value Clover order in the reviewed checkout window. This is not proof of abandonment or a card decline; individual reasons remain unknown unless Clover sends an authoritative result. The checkout-session GET probe returned 404 and is not treated as nonpayment evidence.

Confirmed TNGOS problems:

- Every public Purchase submission created a new order. Retries now use a persisted random request key and server-side payload binding; concurrent retries return the same order. Changed carts receive a new key. Public existing clients remain compatible without the optional key.
- Local-storage exceptions could interrupt checkout after saving an order. Storage restrictions now retain the attempt in memory and do not prevent Clover checkout.
- A Clover checkout request could time out after the provider created it. A durable claim now prevents automatic recreation on uncertain outcomes, while successful requests reuse the saved payment link. Definite 4xx rejections allow an explicit retry; uncertain 5xx, malformed responses, and transport failures require review. No provider response body is exposed as a customer error.
- Signed decline/failure callbacks updated leads but not ticket orders. Matching pending/failed ticket orders now record the provider failure result. A late failure cannot overwrite a paid/refunded order. No tickets are issued from a failure callback.
- Failure returns previously reopened a new blank purchase form; successful returns stopped checking after about 16 seconds. Both return paths now stay on the saved order-status screen and continue polling, moving to tickets only after confirmed paid issuance. Only an authoritative DECLINED record displays “Payment declined.” Unknown results and network errors instruct customers not to pay again. Clover still hosts all card entry and controls its checkout screen.

Staff can download a private customer call list or all unfinished customers from Orders & ticket recovery. The default call list groups attempts by email, excludes customers with another paid purchase, and includes phone/email/order IDs/receipt references and verification guidance. Staff/admin access is required; responses use private no-store and escape spreadsheet formulas. Contact details also display in the support table. Unconfirmed attempt counts are separated from attempts with another paid purchase.

Owner-authorized outreach: six one-time purchase-follow-up emails were accepted by Microsoft 365, including a verified TNG Promotions ticket-page link, a warning not to pay again if already paid, support information, physical address and reply-to-unsubscribe instructions. Customer payment records and opt-outs were rechecked before sending; matching paid customers, invalid email domains and attempts younger than 30 minutes were excluded. Three pending records for one customer received a single email. Durable Microsoft attempt keys prevent replay. Acceptance is not inbox delivery. No SMS was sent: current ticket-delivery consent and the account-notification-only Twilio route do not authorize purchase-promotion reminders. No consent flags were changed.

Validation covers idempotent order creation, payload changes, concurrent PostgreSQL submissions, durable checkout uncertainty, saved checkout reuse, signed decline handling, paid-state preservation, private call exports, CSV safety, browser storage errors, continued status polling, decline versus pending messages, and mobile layout.
