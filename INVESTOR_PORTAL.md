# Investor business portal

Administrators open Investor Center from Administration. Business access is explicit per Investor account; sponsors and concessions accounts do not inherit it. The administrator can preview the same dashboard, add missing business overhead, and publish updates. Existing event-only grants remain scoped to their approved events.

Investor views separate memberships, event summaries, both-corner bout costs, fighter ticket sales, itemized expenses and updates. Membership totals reuse membership evidence and exclude ticket-only customers. Paid ticket receipts exclude customer fees, pending/failed orders and unresolved partial refunds. Known sale refunds reduce reported income. Matching payment references across ticket, sponsor, merchandise and sales sources are deduplicated; exact repeated payment/product rows are flagged.

Event income and costs follow the event date; gym/retail follows sale dates; manually recorded income and expenses follow their recorded dates. Membership counts are current. Event costs include recorded purse/travel/per-diem obligations after deductions, earned commissions, actual shared costs, and the actual state assessment or a clearly labeled 5% estimate. Both corners appear in bout costs; shared-fighter costs across multiple bouts require allocation.

Other business entries must represent costs or paid income not already recorded elsewhere. Entries are versioned, retain history when archived, and do not transfer money. Private admin notes, member/customer identities, billing information, ticket secrets and draft updates are excluded from investor responses. Investor totals are management information, not bank balances, audited profit or investment returns. Missing overhead and unresolved records are flagged rather than invented.

The expense view lists business overhead and event cost components, with a shortcut to itemized fighter purses, allowances, deductions and separate payment states. CSV snapshots provide high-level income and cost summaries.
