# TNG Members privacy notice — draft for owner review

TNG Members is a staff application used by TNG Boxing to provide member profile and attendance services on Clover POS.

Authorized gym staff can access member names, contact information, member numbers, membership status and expiry, waiver-on-file status, QR credentials and recent check-in history. They can update basic contact information and record check-ins. The app does not display payment card numbers or process payments.

Member information is retrieved over encrypted HTTPS from TNG's existing backend. The app does not maintain a member database on the Clover device. A time-limited staff session is stored on that device using Android Keystore encryption and is removed from the app on sign-out. Server-side membership and attendance records remain in TNG's existing system.

Staff access is limited by role. The app's session cannot be used to open unrelated TNG CRM endpoints. Internet connectivity is required; the app does not save check-ins offline for later replay.

For questions about member records, retention, correction or deletion, contact TNG Boxing at marketing@tngboxinggym.com or +1 405-959-5823. The owner should confirm the applicable retention practices and service-provider disclosures before publishing this notice.
