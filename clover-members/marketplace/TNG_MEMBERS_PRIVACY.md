# TNG Members Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026.

TNG Boxing operates TNG Members, a Clover Station Duo application connected to the gym's TNGOS membership system.

## Information and purposes

Authorized gym staff use member names, contact details, member numbers, membership status and expiration, waiver-on-file status, QR credentials, and recent attendance to manage member profiles, confirm membership eligibility, and record check-ins. Staff can correct basic contact information. The customer-facing screen accepts a member number or QR scan and displays limited check-in feedback rather than the member directory.

The application does not process payments or collect payment card numbers. It uses the device's Clover scanner or camera to read member QR codes; the application does not save camera images or video.

## Storage and security

Member records are retrieved over encrypted HTTPS from TNGOS. The application does not maintain a local member database or queue offline check-ins. A time-limited staff session is encrypted with Android Keystore and removed on sign-out. Membership and attendance records remain in the gym's backend system. Access is restricted to authorized staff roles. No method of electronic storage or transmission guarantees absolute security.

## Providers and sharing

TNG uses DigitalOcean to host TNGOS and Clover's platform to operate the device application. Providers process information as needed to deliver these services. The application does not include advertising or sell member information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect rights and security.

Customer mobile numbers and text-message consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. They are shared with providers only as needed to deliver the service. Using the member check-in app does not by itself enroll a member in promotional texts.

## Retention and requests

TNG retains member and attendance records as needed to administer memberships, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Members may contact TNG to request access, corrections, or deletion. TNG may verify identity and retain records where needed for those purposes. Signing out or uninstalling the app removes access on the device but does not delete backend membership records.
