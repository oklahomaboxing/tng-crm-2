Ticket Privacy Notice

Effective October 13, 2026. These policies cover TNG Boxing event ticket purchases and optional ticket texts. Contact: marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823.

Information used for your tickets

We collect your name, email, ticket selections, order and payment references, and the fighter or seller associated with your purchase. If you request ticket texts, we collect your mobile number and record the consent wording and time. We use these records to process purchases, deliver QR tickets, check admission, resolve purchase and delivery issues, and account for seller sales and commissions.

Service providers and access

Clover processes payments, Resend delivers ticket emails, and Twilio delivers optional ticket texts. These providers receive the information needed to provide their services. Authorized event staff can access records needed to support purchases and admission. Keep your private ticket link and QR ticket secure.

Optional ticket texts

Text consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Ticket consent does not enroll you in promotional texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. For help, contact marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823.

Customer mobile numbers and text-message consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share them with service providers only as needed to deliver the service.

Questions and corrections

Contact marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823 with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete your ticket information. We may need to verify your identity and retain records required to administer purchases, resolve disputes, or meet accounting and legal obligations.
