TNG Boxing ticket text consent

Customers choose optional text delivery while purchasing event tickets. No account or login is required. Open the public ticket checkout.

  1. Select the event tickets and enter the buyer's name and email.
  2. To request ticket texts, enter a mobile number and select the separate, initially unchecked consent checkbox shown below.
  3. Continue to Clover payment. Choosing texts is optional; buyers may leave the box unchecked and still purchase tickets and receive email and ticket-page access.

The exact consent notice

I agree to receive TNG Boxing text messages about this ticket purchase, including my QR ticket link and delivery updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

The checkbox is not preselected. Affirmative consent is recorded against the order with the mobile number, notice text/version and time. This ticket campaign does not use verbal, paper, membership, or text-keyword enrollment. Consent is specific to purchased-ticket notifications and delivery updates, not promotional offers.
