TNG Boxing

Ticket Terms

Effective October 13, 2026. These policies cover TNG Boxing event ticket purchases and optional ticket texts. Contact: marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823.

Purchase and admission

Checkout shows your event, ticket selection, quantity, and total before payment through Clover. Tickets are issued after verified successful payment. Each valid QR ticket permits one admission for its event and ticket type. Used, void, or refunded tickets cannot be reused. Keep your ticket and private access link secure. Contact marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823 if a paid ticket is missing.

Ticket delivery and optional texts

Tickets are available on the ticket page and by email. Optional ticket texts require a valid mobile number and your express consent. Texts concern your purchase, including QR ticket links and delivery updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase. For help, contact marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823. Carrier restrictions can affect text delivery; email and the ticket page remain available.

Refunds and event changes

Tickets are nonrefundable when a customer cancels their purchase. If TNG cancels the event, the ticket purchase will be refunded. If TNG reschedules the event, existing tickets remain valid; customers unable to attend the new date may request a refund by contacting marketing@tngboxinggym.com or 405-959-5823.
