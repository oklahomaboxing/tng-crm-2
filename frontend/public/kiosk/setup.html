TNG Boxing — InBody Touch setup

  1. On your staff computer, open TNGOS → Check-In Kiosk → Add check-in device. Name the device and save. Default confirmation is 8 seconds.
  2. Select Generate pairing code. On the InBody touchscreen, open the member check-in kiosk and enter the staff pairing code. It works once and expires in 10 minutes. Keep your administrator login off the entrance screen.
  3. Touch Full screen. In Chrome, use Install app or Add to Home screen if available. Keep Android System WebView/Chrome updated.
  4. Alternatively, with permission from your device administrator, download the separate TNG Check-In Android app. Open the APK in Downloads and allow installation from that source if Android requests it. Disable that installation permission again afterwards. This is separate from InBody and Clover; it does not replace their apps.
  5. Open TNG Check-In and pair it. Browser/PWA and APK storage are separate, so pair whichever you will actually use. Pairing a replacement disconnects the old session.
  6. Try an active staff member number with their permission. Check the Members attendance history. A second immediate check-in should show Already checked in without adding another attendance entry.

The welcome screen also shows a Scan to join TNG Boxing QR code. Visitors scan it with their own phone to open the existing member registration, waiver, membership selection and payment process at tngos.tngboxinggym.com/register. No member data or kiosk credential is included in this QR.

After a restart

Pairing is retained. Tap the TNG Check-In icon after startup. For unattended startup, ask the InBody supplier or your device administrator to configure its supported startup-app or managed kiosk feature to open TNG Check-In. Android 11 does not let ordinary apps guarantee launching themselves after boot. Screen pinning does not provide autostart.

Keep the original InBody software installed and accessible. Do not factory-reset, root the device, replace its launcher or disable InBody services. This downloadable app is not yet tested on your physical InBody firmware.

Daily operation

Members enter the numeric part of their member number using the keypad, keeping any leading zeros. Success is shown for 8 seconds; errors clear after 5 seconds. Next Member clears immediately. Expired or inactive memberships are directed to staff. Fullscreen and screen-awake behavior in a browser depends on device support; the Android app requests both directly.

TNGOS staff can enable/disable the kiosk, adjust timers, see daily check-ins and failed/duplicate attempts, and revoke access. Internet is required; offline attempts are not recorded or queued. “Online” means a heartbeat within 90 seconds.
